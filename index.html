How we work

Software is the second half. The first half is the process.

Most small companies don't have a software problem. They have a process that works, lives entirely in three people's heads, and has never been said out loud. Automating the mess just gets you the mess faster — so we start by finding out how the work actually gets done, who touches it, and where the hours quietly go.

  • We talk to the people doing the job

    Not just the owner. The estimator, the PM, the person who ends up retyping everything at 6pm. They already know where it breaks.

  • We write the process down

    A plain description of how a job goes from call to signed: the steps, the handoffs, the decisions, the things that have to be asked before anyone's on site. Most companies have never had this on one page.

  • We fix what doesn't need software

    Some of the biggest wins are a checklist, a standard question set, or one meeting that happens before kickoff instead of after. If a change works on paper first, it'll work in software.

  • Then we build the part worth building

    The hub enforces the process quietly — the right fields, the right order, the same document every time — so doing it right is the easy path instead of the disciplined one.

The best part of this work is sitting down with somebody and hearing how their job actually goes.

There's almost always more potential in a job than anyone in the building realizes — especially at small companies, where the process lives in people's heads and nobody's ever had a reason to write it down. Get it out on the table and the fixes are usually obvious, cheap, and overdue.

That conversation is where every build here starts. Even if it ends with "you don't need software for this," that's a good outcome and we'll tell you so.

The main thing

Proposal generation

Your team picks the template, fills in the job, and the finished proposal comes out in your company's format and voice — scope language, exclusions, terms, and all. Saved as a draft they can edit, versioned when it changes, and tracked once it goes out.

The AI that drafts the language runs on our servers, under our key. Nothing to install, nothing for your people to configure.

  • 01Pick the template — reroof, siding, multifamily turn, whatever you actually sell.
  • 02Fill in the job: property, scope, quantities, terms.
  • 03The hub drafts it in your voice, on your letterhead.
  • 04Edit, save, send. Status follows it to accepted or declined.
