How we work

We start with the conversation, not the software.

Most of the value shows up before a line of code gets written. We find out how the work really gets done, fix what doesn’t need software, and build only the part worth building.

Software is the second half. The first half is the process.

Most companies your size don’t have a software problem. They have a way of working that gets results, lives in three people’s heads, and has never been said out loud. Putting software on top of a mess just gets you the mess faster. So we start by finding out how the work really gets done, who touches it, and where the hours go.

Written down, a process does three things at once. It gives you hours back every week. It makes the good version of the job repeatable, instead of depending on one person. And it turns your next hire from a six-month ramp into a few weeks. The software is how you make it stick.

  • We talk to the people doing the job

    Not just the owner. The estimator, the project manager, the person retyping everything at six o’clock. They already know where it breaks.

  • We write the process down

    A plain description of how a job goes from first call to signed: the steps, the handoffs, the decisions, the questions that have to be asked before anyone is on site. Most companies have never had this on one page.

  • We fix what doesn’t need software

    Some of the biggest wins are a checklist, a standard set of questions, or one meeting that happens before the job starts instead of after. If a change works on paper, it will work in software.

  • Then we build the part worth building

    The software holds the process quietly: the right fields, in the right order, the same document every time. Doing it right becomes the easy path instead of the disciplined one.

The best part of this work is sitting down with somebody and hearing how their job actually goes.

There is almost always more potential in a job than anyone in the building realizes. Especially at companies where the process lives in people’s heads and nobody has ever had a reason to write it down. Get it out on the table and the fixes are usually obvious, cheap, and overdue.

That conversation is where every build starts. Even if it ends with “you don’t need software for this,” that’s a good outcome and we’ll tell you so.

How we work together

It starts with a conversation, not a contract.

You will know what this costs and what it does before you commit to anything. Nobody builds software for you off a form submission.

01

We sit down with you

A working session with the owner. How the business really runs, what your people spend their days on, where the time goes, and what you want the company to look like in two years.

02

We show you where we’d help

A straight answer on what should be written down, what software would earn its keep, and what you should fix on paper first. Including the parts we’d tell you not to build.

03

You get a quote

Scope and price in writing before anything gets built. You decide what’s worth doing and what waits. No surprise invoices and no open-ended hourly.

04

We build it and keep it running

Something working in front of you early, not at the end. Then hosting, updates, and changes as the business changes. You call, it gets fixed.
