What it costs to make today

Nine site visits, a spreadsheet, and most of a weekend. From a hub it is nine inspection records already in the system, assembled into one document.

Restoration on the four fair roofs: $854,400. Replacing the same four instead: roughly $2.1M. That comparison, in writing, is the argument — and it is the same argument on every portfolio.
